Report Design

As part of my independent learning, I explored how a financial dataset could be presented using Power BI.

A proposed financial performance report could include:

- Total Revenue KPI
- Total Profit KPI
- Average Profit Margin
- Revenue by Company
- Revenue by Month
- Profit by Sector
- Sector filtering using a slicer

This exercise helped develop my understanding of selecting appropriate visualisations for different analytical questions.

Introductory DAX

I explored basic DAX measures that could be used to analyse financial data.

Example:

`Total Revenue = SUM(FinancialData[Revenue])`

Additional introductory calculations explored include total cost, total profit and profit margin.

This also introduced me to the concept of filter context and how measures can respond to filters applied within a report.
